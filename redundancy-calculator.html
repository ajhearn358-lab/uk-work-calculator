About This UK Redundancy Calculator

This estimate assumes you are an eligible employee with at least two years of continuous service. Enhanced contractual redundancy pay, notice pay and holiday pay are separate. Eligibility exceptions can apply. The weekly pay cap is £751 for the supported dates; only the last 20 completed years count. Rates checked 17 September 2026 against GOV.UK.

How Statutory Redundancy Pay Is Calculated

Statutory redundancy pay depends on:

  • Your age
  • Your length of continuous service
  • Your weekly pay (subject to a statutory cap)

The formula applies:

  • 0.5 week’s pay for each full year under age 22
  • 1 week’s pay for each full year aged 22–40
  • 1.5 weeks’ pay for each full year aged 41 and over

Notice Period Entitlement

Employer statutory notice is normally one week after one month of service, then one week per completed year from two years of service, capped at 12 weeks. Your contract may provide more. Notice pay is not included in the redundancy figure above.
